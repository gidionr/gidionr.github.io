About

Background & Identity

Gidion Rabut is a Microsoft Power Platform Solution Architect and Global Client Delivery Partner whose practice runs on a single operating principle: enterprise technology only delivers value when it is governed, architected, and deployed with discipline.

His career spans Big Four advisory, enterprise rail operations, and independent consulting — a cross-industry arc that shapes how he approaches Microsoft Enterprise Cloud engagements. He translates complex organizational requirements into structured, scalable Power Platform solutions, and as a Global Client Delivery Partner, he manages that delivery across client portfolios at scale, maintaining consistency in quality and governance regardless of engagement size or geography.

His delivery focus sits at the intersection of architecture and execution. He designs environment strategies, governs Copilot Studio deployments, architects DLP policies, and builds ALM pipelines — not as isolated workstreams, but as integrated components of a managed delivery framework that client organizations can operate and extend independently after engagement close.

Every engagement carries a commitment to knowledge transfer and long-term client self-sufficiency. Solutions are built to be owned, not supported by a vendor indefinitely.

Outside the consulting practice, Gidion is a co-founder and Publisher of The Kenya Times, an independent digital news publication headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya. The Kenya Times operates with a specific editorial mandate: investigative journalism that holds institutions accountable and surfaces stories that shape public understanding. It is the kind of work he believes technology should serve — not replace.

That conviction connects his two professional lives. He sees responsible tech deployment and quality journalism as complementary forces, both capable of strengthening the institutions communities depend on. It also informs his approach to philanthropy, where his focus falls on initiatives that expand access — to information, to digital infrastructure, and to the tools that allow underserved communities to participate in an increasingly technology-driven economy.
